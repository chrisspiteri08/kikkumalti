Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Information you submit

When you send a partnership enquiry, Kikku receives your name, organisation, email address, selected area of interest and message.

How it is used

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible collaboration. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Storage and sharing

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is useful for the conversation and do not sell it. It is not shared with third parties except where needed to operate the website or comply with the law.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your enquiry information by replying to an email from Kikku.
