- Facebook: `https://kikkumalti.com/ktejjeb/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=free_booklet_launch`
- YouTube: `https://kikkumalti.com/ktejjeb/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic_video&utm_campaign=free_booklet_launch`
- Meta advertising: `https://kikkumalti.com/ktejjeb/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=free_booklet_launch`

English campaign links use `https://kikkumalti.com/en/booklet/` with the same UTM parameters.
